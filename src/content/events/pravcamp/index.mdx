## About PravCamp
PravCamp is a two day hands-on contribution sprint for [**Prav**](https://prav.app/), where students and open source enthusiasts come together to build, fix, and learn by directly contributing to the project. It kicks off right after the PravConf introductory talk and runs across two full days at CET.

## What to Expect
- Guided onboarding to the Prav codebase
- Mentors and maintainers available to help you get your first (or next) contribution merged
- A collaborative, beginner friendly environment
- Certificates and community networking

## Who Should Attend
Anyone from first time contributors to experienced developers are welcome. All you need is a curious mind to learn. No prior experience with Prav is required.
